
# Key Insights

## Overview
The sales dataset was analyzed after cleaning to identify trends, performance drivers, and business patterns.

---

## Key Findings

- Sales show clear variation across months and years, indicating seasonal trends.
- A small number of products contribute significantly to overall revenue.
- Certain regions consistently outperform others in terms of sales.
- Higher discounts tend to reduce profitability, highlighting the need for balanced pricing strategies.
- Customer type influences purchasing behavior and revenue distribution.
- Sales channels (online/offline) impact overall sales performance differently.

---

## Business Implications

- Focus on high-performing products to maximize revenue.
- Optimize discount strategies to protect profit margins.
- Invest more in top-performing regions.
- Tailor strategies based on customer segments and sales channels.

---

## Conclusion

The cleaned dataset enabled reliable analysis and meaningful insights.  
These findings can support better decision-making in sales strategy, pricing, and market focus.
